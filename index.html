5 Key Factors to Consider When Selecting Wires and Cables for Semiconductor Manufacturing

▣ Oct 2026   |   ◷ 5 min read

In semiconductor manufacturing, equipment reliability and operational safety depend heavily on the quality and suitability of wires and cables. Here are 5 key factors to consider when selecting the right wires and cables for your application.

1. Environmental Conditions

Semiconductor manufacturing environments involve cleanrooms, chemical exposure and strict temperature and humidity control. Select wires and cables with appropriate insulation and jacket materials that can withstand the required conditions.

2. Electrical Performance

Ensure the cable can handle the required voltage, current and signal transmission requirements. Confirm conductor configuration and electrical requirements against the equipment design and relevant datasheet.

3. Chemical and Corrosion Resistance

INDUSTRIAL CABLE JACKETS

Cables in semiconductor facilities may be exposed to chemicals, solvents and cleaning agents. Product suitability should be checked against the specific operating environment and approved technical documentation.

4. Compliance and Safety Standards

Where UL certification is required, confirm the certification applicable to the specific product and specification selected. Relevant certification information and product documentation should be reviewed before final selection.
