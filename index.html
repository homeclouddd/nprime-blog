In semiconductor manufacturing, equipment reliability and operational safety depend heavily on the quality and suitability of wires and cables. Here are 5 key factors to consider when selecting the right wires and cables for your application.

1. Environmental Conditions

Semiconductor manufacturing environments involve cleanrooms, chemical exposure and strict temperature and humidity control. Select wires and cables with appropriate insulation and jacket materials that can withstand these conditions, ensuring long-term reliability and safety.

2. Electrical Performance

Ensure the cable can handle the required voltage, current and signal transmission requirements. For sensitive equipment, low noise, low capacitance and stable signal integrity are essential to maintain process accuracy.

3. Chemical and Corrosion Resistance

Cables in semiconductor facilities may be exposed to chemicals, acids, solvents and cleaning agents. Choosing cables with high chemical and corrosion resistance helps prevent degradation and extends the service life.

4. Compliance and Safety Standards

Look for cables that meet relevant industry standards such as UL, RoHS and REACH. Certified cables ensure safety, reliability and compliance with global semiconductor manufacturing requirements.
