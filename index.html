5 Key Factors to Consider When Selecting Industrial Wire and Cable for Semiconductor Equipment

In semiconductor manufacturing, reliable electrical connectivity plays an important role in supporting equipment, control systems and production processes. With different technical and operating requirements across applications, selecting industrial wire and cable should involve more than simply comparing product availability or price.

For procurement managers, electrical engineers, operations managers and manufacturing directors, understanding the application requirements can support a more informed product evaluation. Here are five important factors to consider.

1

Understand the Operating Environment

Start by understanding where and how the cable will be used. Semiconductor manufacturing equipment can have different installation and operating conditions. Factors such as temperature, available installation space, cable movement and routing requirements may affect product suitability.

Providing clear information about the operating environment allows suppliers to better understand the application and identify relevant product options.

2

Review the Electrical Requirements

Different equipment may require different electrical specifications. Engineering and procurement teams should review voltage and current requirements, conductor configuration, cable dimensions, equipment specifications and installation requirements.

The selected cable should always be checked against the relevant technical datasheet and equipment requirements before confirmation.

3

Check Product Compatibility

A suitable cable needs to match the requirements of the equipment and application. Compatibility may include physical dimensions, electrical characteristics, connection requirements and installation conditions.

Providing the part number, required specifications, quantity and intended application can make it easier to identify suitable options and reduce unnecessary back-and-forth during sourcing.

⚙

Preparing an enquiry?

Include the part number, cable specification, required quantity and intended application. This gives the technical and sales team a clearer starting point for product evaluation.

4

Verify UL Certification Requirements

Certification is an important consideration where a project or equipment specification requires UL-certified products. Procurement and engineering teams should confirm the certification applicable to the specific product and specification selected.

Relevant certification information and product documentation should be reviewed before making the final selection.

5

Consider Technical Support and Supplier Responsiveness

Product specifications are only one part of the sourcing process. Access to responsive technical assistance can be useful when teams need additional information to evaluate a product.

Relevant datasheets, application guidance, product information and availability information can help procurement and engineering teams coordinate requirements before requesting a quotation or placing an order.

Key Takeaway

“The right cable selection starts with the application: understand the operating conditions, confirm electrical and compatibility requirements, verify certification and use reliable technical information before making the final decision.”
